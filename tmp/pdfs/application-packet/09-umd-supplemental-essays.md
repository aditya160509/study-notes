## 1. If I could travel anywhere, I would go to…
Platform 9¾ at King's Cross, then the studio where Harry Potter was filmed. I know it is a wall with half a trolley stuck in it. I would still run at it. I grew up borrowing Harry Potter quotes for everything from exams to football losses, and I want to stand in the Great Hall set and see whether it feels as big as it did in my head. Then I'd find the nearest South Indian restaurant, because no trip is complete without idli-sambar.

## 2. The most interesting fact I ever learned from research was…
that in Turkey, the stock market barely follows attention from the rest of the world. Across 21 markets, Borsa Istanbul had the second-lowest share of attention surges preceded by foreign markets: about 10%, against 28% for its peers. What does predict a Turkish surge is the lira falling overnight, before the market even opens. I expected foreign investors to drive it. The data said the story was local.

## 3. In addition to my major, my academic interests include…
behavioral economics, because every system I build eventually runs into people copying people: investors follow trades, and club members follow the most confident answer in the room. I also love probability puzzles. The ten-door Monty Hall game is my favorite way to turn a quiet room into an argument, and I want to study the psychology of why people refuse to switch.

## 4. My favorite thing about last Tuesday was…
the call. Four of us in one group chat, no agenda. Someone starts a GeoGuessr round and we argue over which country a blurry road belongs to, then switch to Minecraft and build something nobody planned. If a match is on, we watch together and our commentary gets worse than the football. Nobody mentions exams or papers. By the time I hang up, it is well past my usual 10:30 and I have learned nothing useful, which is exactly why it was my favorite part of the day.

## 5. Something you might not know about me is…
that I will travel an unreasonable distance and wait an unreasonable time for one plate of idli-sambar at Dakshinayan in Mumbai: an hour of traffic, another hour in line. My family considers this a normal Sunday. I also sleep by 10:30 and wake up without an alarm, which sounds disciplined until you see how often I lose my keys.

## 6. Diversity (650 chars): "…describe how you have learned, grown, been inspired or developed skills through one or more components of diversity."
Through teaching children in villages near my school, I learned that being correct is not the same as being understood. They had different schooling, different daily lives, and no reason to listen to an outsider. My clear explanations got polite nods. So my friend and I stopped lecturing and used teams, games, and examples from their lives. One boy who had avoided every question raised his hand for algebra because getting it right meant beating his friend. Now, before I explain anything, I ask what the other person already knows. The gap between us is information I need, not something to talk over.
