## Q1-M1 — Capacitor Discharge I–t (9702/52/F/M/20) — $I = \frac{E}{R}e^{-t/RC}$

**Equation & Graph:** $I = \frac{E}{R} e^{-t/RC}$ → $\ln I = -t/RC + \ln(E/R)$ → plot $\ln I$ (y) vs $t$ (x) → gradient $-1/RC$, intercept $\ln(E/R)$.

**Key marks:** Use ammeter + stopwatch, measure $I$ at fixed $t$ intervals, keep $R,C,E$ constant, discharge fully between runs, plot $\ln I$ vs $t$.

---

## Q1-M2 — Thermistor R–T (9702/52/O/N/20) — $R = R_0 e^{B/T}$

**Equation:** $\ln R = B/T + \ln R_0$ → $\ln R$ vs $1/T$.

**Key marks:** Thermistor in water bath, thermometer, ohmmeter, vary $T$ via hot water, keep current low to avoid self-heating.

---

## Q1-M3 — Heated Wire + Extension (9702/51/M/J/19) — $y \propto I^2$

**Equation:** $y = k I^2$ → $y$ vs $I^2$ through origin.

**Key marks:** Same wire + pointer + scale + weight as Q1-3 pure, but now $I$ is independent, $y$ measured, control length/diameter.

---

## Q2-M4 — Strain Gauge $V$–$\Delta L$ (9702/52/M/J/19 Q2 analogue)

**Equation:** $V \propto \Delta L$ → $V$ vs $\Delta L$ through origin.

**Key marks:** Gauge bonded to specimen, Wheatstone bridge, micrometer for $\Delta L$, keep temperature constant.
