## 3) Premium Forwards

### Erling Haaland — Man City, £15.5m — **ESSENTIAL**
Most expensive asset in FPL history; **75.2% owned** and climbing. Top FPL scorer 2 of last 4 seasons incl. 2025/26 (239 pts, 27 goals); top-scoring player after 6 GWs in *all four* of his City seasons (avg 9 goals, 61.3 pts in that span). Productive World Cup with Norway. Hosts Bournemouth GW1 (4 goals in his last 4 vs them; Iraola gone, Senesi sold). Coventry + Ipswich at home in first 7 → Triple Captain candidate. Going without is a massive rank risk.

### João Pedro — Chelsea, £7.5m — **ESSENTIAL**
Same price as last season despite 15g/9a (177 pts). Hat-trick + assist in Alonso's first pre-season game; 6+ pre-season goals. No European football, skipped the World Cup = fully rested. **Second most-selected player in the game (>50%)**. Opens at Fulham, then Brighton (H), Hull (H). The consensus Haaland strike partner.

### Alexander Isak — Liverpool, £9.0m — **GOOD PICK**
£6.5m cheaper than Haaland as Liverpool's lead striker. Salah's departure refocuses the whole Liverpool attack. Konate's exit slightly weakens the defence behind him. Solid but no Haaland.

### Thiago — Brentford, price TBC (was £7.2m end-24/25; expect ~£8m+) — **GOOD PICK**
22 goals (9 pens) and 181 pts last season — best value forward in the game. Penalties + a kind opening run (Burnley H first per FDR tables) keep him relevant, but regression after a career year is fair to assume.

### Viktor Gyökeres — Arsenal, ~£9m band (unconfirmed) — **SITUATIONAL**
Only 14 league goals in Arsenal's title season (128 pts) — fine, not elite. New signing Tzolis is outscoring him in pre-season chatter. Title favourites' striker always has a floor; wait for evidence he's first-choice penalty/lead role.

### Ollie Watkins — Aston Villa, ~£8.5–9m — **SITUATIONAL**
16 goals last season but Villa lost Rogers (£117m) and their attack needs rebuilding. GW1 fixture per live listings reads Man City (A) in some sources, Burnley (H) in others — verify before deadline. Hold unless fixtures confirm kind.

### Yoane Wissa — Newcastle, ~£7.5m band — **SITUATIONAL**
Inherits the No.9 role with Gordon sold and Isak-era money spent; Newcastle have the **easiest opening FDR in the league (2.50 avg)**: Sunderland (H), Burnley (A), Leeds (H). Real upside if priced ≤£7.5m.

## 4) Premium Midfielders

### Bruno Fernandes — Man Utd, £12.0m — **ESSENTIAL**
235 pts (2nd overall), record **24 assists**, 136 key passes, 33 big chances created. Under Carrick since GW22: 129 pts in 17 games (7.6/match), returns in 14 of 17. Penalties + set pieces + 90-min reliability. Most-owned midfielder (48.8%). Opens vs promoted Hull then Ipswich — genuine rival to Haaland for the armband.

### Cole Palmer — Chelsea, £9.5m — **GOOD PICK (not essential GW1)**
Down year (114 pts) after back-to-back 200+ seasons; price cut £1.0m to £9.5m. No Europe, new manager bounce possible, but missed a pre-season friendly with injury and Rogers' arrival muddies roles. Consensus view: viable GW1, but many are planning Bruno→Palmer around GW4. Fine to start with; don't force it.

### Bukayo Saka — Arsenal, £9.5m — **SITUATIONAL (GW1), GOOD PICK after**
Elite points-per-minute when fit (157 pts in an injury-hit season). But: deep World Cup run, ongoing Achilles concern, nursing a knock — Arteta says he's "ready to be involved," which is not "ready to start." £9.5m is too much for a bench risk on deadline day. Strong buy once fitness is proven.

### Antoine Semenyo — Man City, £8.5m — **GOOD PICK**
202 pts and 17 goals last season; 5 pre-season assists under Maresca. Cheaper route into the Haaland attack — pairing both is a legitimate strategy. Only doubt is City minutes; if he starts GW1, he's worth it immediately.

### Florian Wirtz — Liverpool, £7.5m — **GOOD PICK**
Underwhelming debut season (5g/4a) means he's £1.0m cheaper than his 25/26 start price. With Salah gone, the Liverpool attack runs through him if used centrally. RotoWire calls him one of the three non-negotiable premium mids of 26/27 alongside Palmer and the City spearhead. Calculated gamble with big upside.

### Morgan Gibbs-White — Nott'm Forest, £8.0m — **SITUATIONAL**
Highest-scoring midfielder of 25/26 (188 pts, 15 goals) but brutal early run: Leeds, then Liverpool, Spurs, Villa, Arsenal. Watchlist, not GW1.

### Declan Rice — Arsenal, £7.5m — **GOOD PICK**
184 pts (4g/9a + DefCon) — steady accumulator in the best team. Fitness flag post-World Cup ("ready to be involved"). Safer than Wirtz, lower ceiling.

### Morgan Rogers — Chelsea, £7.5m — **SITUATIONAL**
British-record £117m fee yet priced like a mid-picker. Ceiling is enormous next to Palmer, but early role uncertain and he's easing back after England duty. One to buy once he's clearly starting.

### Bryan Mbeumo — Man Utd, £8.0m — **GOOD PICK**
Used centrally in pre-season under Carrick; elite npxG involvement last year. Hull/Ipswich opening pair makes him a near-mid-premium at £8.0m. Matheus Cunha (£8.0m) is the alternative route into the same attack.

### Dominik Szoboszlai — Liverpool, £7.0m — **GOOD PICK**
Started 36/38 last season (6g/7a + DefCon + set pieces). Minutes security is gold at this price; excellent opening fixtures.

## 5) Premium Defenders + Keepers

### David Raya — Arsenal, £6.0m — **GOOD PICK (best GK)**
First £6.0m keeper in years and justified: reigning Golden Glove behind the league's best defence. Dominant Arsenal matches limit save volume, but CS probability is unmatched. Alisson and Donnarumma are the other £5.5m+ names; Pickford the high-volume alternative.

### Gabriel — Arsenal, £8.0m — **ESSENTIAL (if budget allows)**
209 pts in only 30 starts — third-highest defender total in FPL history. 18 CS, 3 goals, 5 assists, 22 DefCon points; blanked in just 6 of 30 starts. 7.0 pts/start home, 6.8 away — on par with Haaland/Bruno pps. Play every week, never captain. The only £8.0m defender in the game.

### Virgil van Dijk — Liverpool, £6.5m — **GOOD PICK**
175 pts, 6 goals (most among defenders), 30 DefCon. Liverpool conceded 53 but underlying numbers were better; fixtures friendly (Newcastle A, Forest H, Ipswich A, Fulham H). Safest route into a rebounding Liverpool defence.

### Nico O'Reilly — Man City, £6.5m — **GOOD PICK (high ceiling)**
5g/4a last season; highest npxG/90 (0.21) among £6.5m defenders; played as an extra midfielder in the Community Shield. Five home bankers in first 10 GWs. Risk: City rotation (Aït-Nouri competes). High risk, high reward.

### Jurriën Timber — Arsenal, £6.5m — **AVOID (GW1) / GOOD later**
Groin injury, expected back September/October. Great asset when fit — just not tonight.

### Marc Guéhi — Man City, £6.0m — **SITUATIONAL**
179 pts last season (2nd-best defender) but hasn't played in pre-season and didn't start the Community Shield; Gvardiol competition. Upside play only once minutes are confirmed.

### James Tarkowski — Everton, £6.0m — **GOOD PICK**
DefCon threshold hit 22 times (10.19 CBIT/90), 2g/3a, 11 CS. Boring, bankable floor.

### Marcos Senesi — Tottenham, £6.0m — **SITUATIONAL**
DefCon elite (11.39 CBIT/90) but De Zerbi's possession Spurs may cut his defensive actions; CS upside compensates. Watch.

### Riccardo Calafiori — Arsenal, £5.5m — **GOOD PICK (value star)**
The affordable way into the Arsenal defence with attacking threat down the left; hosts Coventry GW1. Best £5.5m defender alongside Sunderland's Nordi Mukiele (3g/5a last season, faces Ipswich).

### Keepers 5.0m and below (context): Kinsky (Spurs, £4.5m) has won the job after a squad reshuffle; Lammens (Man Utd, ~£5.0m) is the value pick with United's kind start; Dubravka cheap at a promoted-side-beating club.
