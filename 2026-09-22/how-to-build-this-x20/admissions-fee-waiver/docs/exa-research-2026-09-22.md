# Admissions research log — 22 September 2026

Exa reviewed 111 results across seven workstreams. Only official university, campus, financial-aid, UCAS, or Common App sources were accepted for application facts. Graduate, law, medical, transfer, commercial-adviser, and stale-cycle pages were excluded unless explicitly labeled as the latest published campus information.

## Confirmed high-signal findings

- Princeton supports a Common App fee-waiver request with counselor approval, or a Princeton-specific waiver for qualifying applicants. Source: https://admission.princeton.edu/apply/application-checklist
- Yale directs applicants to the fee-waiver mechanism in their chosen application platform. Source: https://admissions.yale.edu/counselors
- Stanford's first-year application fee is $100 and it provides a fee-waiver request route. Source: https://admission.stanford.edu/apply/first-year/fee.html
- Caltech's first-year application fee is $85 or an approved waiver. Source: https://www.admissions.caltech.edu/apply/first-year-applicants/application-requirements
- Carnegie Mellon accepts Common App economic-need indicators plus College Board and NACAC waivers. Source: https://www.cmu.edu/admission/admission/undergraduate-admission-requirements
- Penn's first-year fee is $75 and requests go through the Common App or Coalition Application. Source: https://admissions.upenn.edu/how-to-apply/first-year-applicants/application-requirements
- Cornell's first-year waiver is requested in both the Common App profile and Cornell questions. Source: https://admissions.cornell.edu/how-to-apply/first-year-applicants
- Duke permits qualifying students to request a Common App waiver, with counselor endorsement. Source: https://admissions.duke.edu/apply/
- Brown's first-year fee is $80 or a waiver; its undergraduate office handles waiver questions. Source: https://admission.brown.edu/apply/how-apply
- WashU publishes application fee waivers for applicants seeking need-based financial aid. Source: https://admissions.washu.edu/high-school-counselors/
- UCAS equal-consideration for the tracked 2027 UK courses is 13 January 2027; the published application fee should be rechecked when the live 2027 application is submitted. Source: https://www.strath.ac.uk/studywithus/undergraduate/howtoapply/
- Trinity's international applications for September 2027 open in November. Source: https://www.tcd.ie/study/international/
- Aberdeen Mumbai currently publishes an application-fee step and a Pioneer Scholarship; the visible scholarship terms refer to the prior intake and must not be assumed for 2027. Sources: https://www.abdn.ac.uk/mumbai/admissions/ and https://www.abdn.ac.uk/mumbai/admissions/aberdeen-mumbai-scholarships/
- Illinois Tech Mumbai publishes `mumbai-admissions@illinoistech.edu` as its admissions contact and a latest-published ₹5,000 undergraduate application fee. Source: https://www.iit.edu/mumbai/admissions/undergraduate-admissions-and-degree-requirements
- Bristol Mumbai publishes a ₹2,000 application fee that was temporarily waived through 31 August 2026. This is not evidence of a 2027 waiver. Source: https://www.mumbai.bristol.ac.uk/admissions/

## Data rules used in the app

1. A blank email means no official undergraduate contact was verified from the accepted source set.
2. “Not published” is different from “no deadline” or “no fee.”
3. A prior-cycle India-campus fee or scholarship is labeled as latest published, not carried forward as a 2027 fact.
4. Fee-waiver eligibility is never inferred from the student's profile.
5. The live application portal overrides this research log.
