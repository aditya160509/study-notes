# Template 08 — Interactive Listening Summary (75 seconds)

**Task:** After a conversation, write a summary in **75 seconds**.
**Goal:** 3 sentences, 45–70 words, covering who/topic, key detail, outcome.

## During the conversation: capture five things

You cannot take notes on paper. Keep this map in your head.

| Element | Question | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Who** | Who are you talking to? | professor, classmate, manager |
| **Topic** | What is the conversation about? | choosing a job vs a doctorate |
| **Detail 1** | What is one option/concern? | jobs require experience |
| **Detail 2** | What is the other option/advice? | a doctorate builds research experience |
| **Outcome** | What was decided/recommended? | work first, then decide about a doctorate |

## 75-second summary skeleton

```text
[SENTENCE 1 — WHO + TOPIC]
I spoke to ______________ about ______________.

[SENTENCE 2 — KEY DETAIL]
He/She explained that ______________, and suggested that ______________.

[SENTENCE 3 — OUTCOME]
In the end, we agreed that ______________. / The main outcome was ______________.
```

## Alternative frame (if the conversation is more complex)

```text
I had a conversation with ______________ about ______________.
The main issue was ______________. ______________ suggested ______________ because ______________.
As a result, ______________.
```

## Time budget

| Time | Action |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | Decide the three sentences |
| 0:10–1:05 | Write |
| 1:05–1:15 | Check verb tense, articles, spelling |

## Phrase bank

**Opening**
- I spoke to … about …
- I had a conversation with … regarding …
- The conversation was about …

**Reporting**
- He/She explained that …
- He/She pointed out that …
- He/She suggested that …
- He/She recommended …
- According to …

**Outcome**
- In the end, …
- As a result, …
- We agreed that …
- The main conclusion was …
- The takeaway was …

## Model (58 words)

> I spoke to my professor about whether I should apply for a job or a doctoral program after graduation. She explained that most jobs in my field require experience, and she suggested that I work for a few years before deciding whether to pursue a doctorate. In the end, I agreed to gain some professional experience first and reconsider a doctoral program later.

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Retelling every line | Three sentences: who/topic, detail, outcome |
| No outcome | Say what was decided or recommended |
| Wrong tense | Use past tense for the conversation |
| "He said me…" | "He told me…" / "He said that…" |
| Adding your opinion | Summarise, do not argue |
| Writing under 30 words | Aim for 45–70 |

## Checklist

- [ ] I named **who** I spoke to.
- [ ] I named the **topic**.
- [ ] I included one or two **key details**.
- [ ] I stated the **outcome**.
- [ ] I used **past tense** consistently.
- [ ] I wrote **three sentences**.
- [ ] I checked spelling and punctuation.
