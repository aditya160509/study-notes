# Template 10 — Listen and Type (3 plays)

**Task:** Hear one sentence and type it exactly. Up to **three plays**. About **9 items**.
**Goal:** Capture every word, including small function words.

## The 3-play protocol

| Play | Action |
|---|---|
| **1** | Type everything you hear, even if it is full of gaps. Do not stop to fix spelling. |
| **2** | Fill the gaps; check articles, prepositions, plurals, tense endings. |
| **3** | Compare word by word; fix punctuation and capitalisation. |

Do not use play 1 to write a perfect sentence. Use play 1 to **get the shape**, plays 2–3 to **repair**.

## What to listen for

| Feature | Examples | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Articles | *a, an, the* | Often swallowed; easy to miss |
| Prepositions | *in, on, at, of, to* | Change meaning; often unstressed |
| Plurals | *friend / friends* | Final -s is quiet |
| Verb endings | *walk / walked / walking* | -ed and -ing carry tense |
| Contractions | *there's, don't, aren't you* | Both contracted and full forms are usually accepted; be consistent |
| Question tags | *…, aren't you?* | Need a comma and question mark |
| Names/numbers | *Greenland, 1980* | Capitalise proper nouns |

## Punctuation checklist

- Capital letter at the start.
- Full stop at the end of a statement.
- Question mark at the end of a question.
- Comma before a question tag: *You are a teacher, aren't you?*
- Comma after an introductory clause: *Despite graduating years ago, he still …*
- Comma in a list: *old medicine, lotions, and soaps.*

## Repair routine

1. Read your sentence silently.
2. Ask: **subject + verb** — do they agree?
3. Ask: **is every noun countable/uncountable in the right form?**
4. Ask: **did I hear a final -s, -ed or -ing?**
5. Ask: **does the punctuation match the sentence type?**

## Common errors

| Error | Correction |
|---|---|
| Missing *the* | *the university email account* |
| Missing plural | *several criticisms* |
| Wrong tense | *He has written* (not *He have written*) |
| Missing comma | *If they don't want to do it, give them…* |
| No question mark | *You are a teacher, aren't you?* |
| Writing a paraphrase | Type the **exact** words, not your summary |
