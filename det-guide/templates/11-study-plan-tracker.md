## Rules

- **One timed task every day.** Untimed practice alone does not build pacing.
- **Review every error** in [12-error-log.md](12-error-log.md). A mistake you do not log will repeat.
- **One full mock per week**, not more. Mock fatigue is real.
- **Do not learn a new grammar topic in the last 48 hours.** Fix timing and your two weakest tasks.
