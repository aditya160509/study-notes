# Template 01 — Write About the Photo (60 seconds)

**Task:** One photo, **60 seconds**, full sentences. Appears **3 times**; aim for **2–3 sentences**.
**Goal:** 2–3 sentences, 35–55 words, accurate and specific.

## Time budget

| Time | Action |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | Look at the main subject and the setting |
| 0:10–0:45 | Write 2–3 sentences |
| 0:45–1:00 | Check articles, plurals, verb endings, spelling |

## Skeleton

```text
[1. SUMMARY] This photo shows ______________ (who) ______________ (doing what) in/at ______________ (place).

[2. ACTION] ______________ is/are ______________ing ______________, while ______________ is/are ______________ing ______________.

[3. LOCATION] In the foreground / background / on the left / on the right / behind ______________, there is/are ______________.

[OPTIONAL 4. DETAIL] ______________ looks ______________ because ______________.
```

## Sentence starters

**Summary**
- This photo shows …
- The image shows …
- This is a photograph of …
- The picture captures …

**Action** (present continuous)
- … is sitting / standing / walking / holding / looking at / talking to …
- … are gathered around / waiting for / working on …
- … appears to be …ing …
- … seems to be …ing …

**Location**
- In the foreground, …
- In the background, …
- On the left/right side of the image, …
- In the middle of the photo, …
- Behind / in front of / next to / opposite …
- In the distance, …
- At the top/bottom of the photo, …

**Detail**
- … is wearing …
- The weather looks … because …
- The atmosphere seems … because …
- … looks … , which suggests …

## Model (55 words)

> This photo shows a busy outdoor market with several vendors standing behind fruit stalls. A woman in a blue apron is arranging oranges while a customer is pointing at a crate of apples. In the foreground, there are bright red tomatoes stacked in wooden boxes, and in the background, a row of white buildings lines the narrow street.

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Listing: "There is a man. There is a table. There are apples." | Connect with actions and locations |
| "The photo is show…" | "The photo shows…" |
| Past tense: "A woman was standing…" | Use present tenses: "A woman is standing…" |
| Over-speculation: "They are arguing about money." | "They appear to be discussing something." |
| One long sentence with 4 ideas | One idea per sentence |

## Checklist

- [ ] I answered **what the photo is about** in sentence 1.
- [ ] I described at least **one action** with present continuous.
- [ ] I used at least **one prepositional phrase** for location.
- [ ] I used at least **two descriptive adjectives/adverbs**.
- [ ] I wrote **full sentences**, not labels.
- [ ] I wrote **at least 2 full sentences** (practice target; 3 if time).
- [ ] I checked spelling, articles, plurals and final -s/-ed.
- [ ] I did not invent a story beyond the photo.
