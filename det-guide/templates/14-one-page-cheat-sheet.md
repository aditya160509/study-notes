# Template 14 — One-Page Cram Sheet

Print this or keep it open on your phone. Nothing here replaces a timed practice answer.

## Photo writing — 60 s × 3

```text
1. This photo shows ______ (who) ______ing in/at ______.
2. ______ is/are ______ing ______, while ______ is/are ______ing ______.
3. In the foreground/background/on the left, there is/are ______.
4. Optional: ______ looks ______ because ______.
```

**Rules:** 2–3 full sentences · present tenses · adjectives/adverbs · location prepositions · no lists · no invented stories.

## Photo speaking — 20 s + 90 s

```text
0–15   Summary: This photo shows…
15–40  Actions: … is/are …ing …
40–55  Location: In the foreground/background…
55–75  Speculation: If I had to guess, … because…
75–90  Detail: What stands out is…
```

**Freeze?** Go clockwise: top left → top right → bottom right → bottom left.

## Interactive Writing — 5 min + 3 min

```text
[Topic] I agree/disagree that ______ for two main reasons.
[Reason 1] Firstly, ______. This is because ______.
[Example] For instance, ______.
[Reason 2] Another reason is that ______. As a result, ______.
[Conclusion] In conclusion, ______.
```

**Part 2:** direct answer → link to part 1 → one new detail → close.
**Time:** part 1 write to 4:00, proofread to 5:00; part 2 write to 2:30, proofread to 3:00.

## Writing Sample — 5 min

Same 3-part paragraph. Identify **argument / describe / recount** first. 140–180 words. It goes to universities.

## Read Then Speak — 20 s + 90 s

```text
Q1: One ______ I'd like to talk about is ______.
Present: These days, ______.
Past: I first ______ when ______. I remember ______.
Q2: What made it ______ was ______.
Future: In the future, I plan to ______ because ______.
Q3: That is why ______.
```

**PPF = Present → Past → Future.** Answer **all** sub-questions.

## Interactive Speaking — 35 s × 6–8

```text
O  Opinion: I think ______.
R  Reason: One main reason is that ______.
E  Example: For example, ______.
O  Outcome: As a result, ______.
```

**25–35 seconds.** Click Record. New angle each question. No repetition.

## Speaking Sample — 3 min

PPF or Point → Reason → Example → Conclusion. Specific detail. University-appropriate. No memorised speech.

## Interactive Listening summary — 75 s

```text
I spoke to ______ about ______.
He/She explained that ______ and suggested that ______.
In the end, we agreed that ______.
```

**Capture:** who · topic · detail · outcome.

## Interactive Reading — 7 or 8 min

- Read title + first + last sentence.
- Q1 (choose words): **max 2 min**.
- Highlight **only the answer phrase**.
- Eliminate options with details not in the passage.
- Main idea/title: broad enough, no invented detail.

## Listen and Type

1. Play 1: type everything, gaps allowed.
2. Play 2: fill gaps, check articles/prepositions/plurals/endings.
3. Play 3: punctuation and capitalisation.

## Universal checks

- [ ] Did I answer the **exact** question?
- [ ] Did I use the **full time**?
- [ ] Did I leave **30–60 seconds** to proofread?
- [ ] Did I vary vocabulary and linking words?
- [ ] Did I avoid a memorised answer?
- [ ] Did I check articles, plurals, verb endings, spelling?
