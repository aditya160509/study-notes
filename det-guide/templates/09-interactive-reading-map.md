# Template 09 — Interactive Reading Map (7 or 8 minutes)

**Task:** Two passages; one has **7 minutes**, one has **8 minutes**; **6 questions per passage**.
**Goal:** Bank the easy points before the clock runs out.

## Time budget

| Time | Task |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:40 | Read title, first sentence, last sentence |
| 0:40–2:40 | Q1: choose the correct words (longest) |
| 2:40–3:30 | Q2: choose the linking sentence |
| 3:30–5:15 | Q3–Q4: highlight the exact answers |
| 5:15–6:30 | Q5–Q6: main idea + title |
| 6:30–8:00 | Check the two answers you were unsure about |

If your passage has the **7-minute** timer, use this compressed budget:

| Time | Task |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:35 | Read title, first sentence, last sentence |
| 0:35–2:20 | Q1: choose the correct words |
| 2:20–3:05 | Q2: choose the linking sentence |
| 3:05–4:35 | Q3–Q4: highlight the exact answers |
| 4:35–5:40 | Q5–Q6: main idea + title |
| 5:40–7:00 | Check the two answers you were unsure about |

Do **not** spend more than 2 minutes on question 1. The remaining five questions are quick points.

## Passage map

Fill this in mentally as you read.

```text
Topic: ______________
Who: ______________
Where: ______________
Problem / change: ______________
Outcome: ______________
```

The passage does not change between questions. Do not reread all of it.

## The six question moves

| # | Move | Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Choose the correct word for each gap | Read around the gap; use collocation and grammar; leave a gap and return if unsure |
| 2 | Select the sentence that links two parts | Match **topic + tone**; eliminate options about things not in the passage |
| 3 | Highlight the text that answers question A | Highlight **only the answer phrase**, not the whole sentence |
| 4 | Highlight the text that answers question B | A second, separate answer; do not reuse the first highlight |
| 5 | Select the main idea | Eliminate options with details that are not central |
| 6 | Choose the best title | The title must cover the **whole** passage, not one paragraph |

## Question 1: choosing words

Look for:

- **Collocation:** *widely acclaimed*, *take a turn*, *a sense of clarity*
- **Part of speech:** noun after *a/an/the*, adjective before a noun, verb after a subject
- **Parallel structure:** *playful and fluffy* → both adjectives
- **Pronoun reference:** *he/she/it/they* must match the noun before it
- **Tense consistency:** past narrative stays past

If two options fit grammatically, choose the one that fits the **story**, not the one that sounds more advanced.

## Highlight questions

Ask: **What is the smallest piece of text that answers this?**

- Question: *Why did his sister bring him a surprise?*
- Wrong: highlight the whole sentence about his sister.
- Right: highlight **"worried about his well-being"**.

## Elimination rules

An option is wrong if it:

- mentions a person, place or object **not in the passage**
- is **too narrow** (one detail, not the main idea)
- is **too extreme** (*always, never, all*) when the passage is cautious
- contradicts the passage's **outcome**

## Checklist

- [ ] I read the title and the first/last sentences first.
- [ ] I spent **≤2 minutes** on question 1.
- [ ] I highlighted only the answer phrase.
- [ ] I eliminated options with invented details.
- [ ] I answered every question; no blanks.
- [ ] I left 60–90 seconds to check.
