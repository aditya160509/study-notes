# Template 02 — Speak About the Photo (90 seconds)

**Task:** One photo, **20 seconds prep**, **90 seconds speaking**. Appears **once**.
**Goal:** 5 connected steps, 150–220 words, no long silence.

## Time budget

| Time | Step | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 0–15 s | Summary | What is the photo about? |
| 15–40 s | Actions | Who is doing what? |
| 40–55 s | Location | Where are the main things? |
| 55–75 s | Speculation | A reasonable guess from visible evidence |
| 75–90 s | Detail / wrap-up | Clothing, weather, mood, atmosphere |

The order is flexible after the summary. If you blank, go **clockwise**: top left → top right → bottom right → bottom left.

## Skeleton

```text
[SUMMARY]
This photo shows ______________ (who) ______________ (doing what) in/at ______________ (place).
It looks like ______________ (type of place / occasion).

[ACTIONS]
In the middle of the image, ______________ is/are ______________ing ______________.
Near ______________, ______________ is/are ______________ing ______________.
You can also see ______________ , who/which is ______________ing ______________.

[LOCATION]
In the foreground, ______________.
In the background, ______________.
On the left/right, ______________.
Behind / next to / opposite / above ______________, there is/are ______________.

[SPECULATION]
If I had to guess, I'd say ______________ because ______________.
It seems likely that ______________ , since ______________.
They might be ______________ , although it's hard to tell.

[DETAIL / WRAP-UP]
What stands out most is ______________.
The overall mood feels ______________ because ______________.
```

## Speculation: safe vs unsafe

| Safe (visible evidence) | Unsafe (invented story) |
|---|---|
| "She is probably preparing for an exam because she is surrounded by books." | "Her parents forced her to study medicine instead of music." |
| "They seem to be tourists because they are carrying a map and a camera." | "They are on their honeymoon in Italy." |
| "The shop looks closed because the lights are off and the chairs are stacked." | "The owner went bankrupt last month." |

**Rule:** every guess must point back to something you can see.

## Useful phrases

**Hedging**
- It looks like …
- It seems / appears to be …
- I'd say / I'd guess …
- It might be … , but I'm not sure.
- Judging by … , …

**Adding detail**
- What stands out is …
- I also noticed …
- Another detail is …
- In the distance, you can just make out …
- The colours are mostly …

**Describing mood**
- The atmosphere feels calm / tense / lively / relaxed.
- It gives the impression of …
- The lighting makes the scene look …

## Model opening (first 30 seconds)

> This photo shows a small street-food cart on a rainy street, probably in a Southeast Asian city. In the middle of the image, a man in a dark jacket is pushing the cart from left to right. The cart has a large blue umbrella on top, and there are colourful menu signs attached to the front. On the left, I can see a red mailbox and a folded umbrella, while several shops line the right side of the street.

## If you freeze

1. Say: **"Looking more closely at the photo…"**
2. Describe the nearest object.
3. Move clockwise.
4. Add a colour, material or weather detail.
5. Make one grounded guess.

## Checklist

- [ ] I started with a general summary, not a list.
- [ ] I used present tenses throughout.
- [ ] I described at least **three actions**.
- [ ] I used at least **three prepositional phrases**.
- [ ] I made one **grounded** speculation.
- [ ] I added one bonus detail (clothing/weather/mood).
- [ ] I spoke for **80–90 seconds**.
- [ ] I did not stop to apologise or restart.
