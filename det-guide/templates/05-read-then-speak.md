# Template 05 — Read Then Speak (20 s + 90 s)

**Task:** Read a short prompt, **20 seconds prep**, then speak for up to **90 seconds**.
**Goal:** Answer **all parts** of the question, using **PPF** on one idea. 150–220 words.

## Step 1 — Decode the prompt in 20 seconds

Most prompts have a topic plus **three sub-questions**.

Example:

> Describe a memorable journey you have taken.
> - Where did you go?
> - What happened during the journey?
> - Why was it memorable?

Write three mental slots:

```text
Q1: ______________
Q2: ______________
Q3: ______________
```

If the prompt gives no sub-questions, create your own three angles: **what, why, how it changed you**.

## Step 2 — Choose one idea, not three

Pick **one** journey, habit, person or experience. Apply **PPF** to it:

| Part | What to say |
|---|---|
| **Present** | What the experience/idea is; why it matters now |
| **Past** | A specific memory or detail from before/during |
| **Future** | What you plan to do, or how it will affect you |

This gives length and grammatical range without needing new ideas.

## Step 3 — 90-second skeleton

```text
[Q1 — ANSWER + PRESENT]
One ______________ I'd like to talk about is ______________.
It is ______________. These days, ______________.

[PAST DETAIL]
I first ______________ when ______________.
I remember ______________. At the time, ______________.

[Q2 — DETAIL + EXAMPLE]
What made it ______________ was ______________.
For example, ______________.

[FUTURE / Q3]
In the future, I plan to ______________ because ______________.
Looking back, ______________.

[WRAP-UP]
So, that is why ______________ is so important to me.
```

## Transition phrases for multi-part prompts

- *As for the second part of the question, …*
- *Moving on to ______, …*
- *To answer the final part, …*
- *On the question of ______, …*
- *As I mentioned earlier, …*

## PPF phrase bank

**Present**
- These days, I usually …
- At the moment, I …
- What I enjoy most is …
- It is a big part of my life because …

**Past**
- I first started … when I was …
- I remember one time when …
- At the time, I felt …
- Back then, I used to …

**Future**
- In the future, I plan to …
- I would like to … because …
- Hopefully, I will …
- Looking ahead, …

## Model (90 seconds)

**Prompt:** Describe something you do to stay healthy.
- What do you do?
- How often do you do it?
- Why is it important to you?

> One thing I do to stay healthy is yoga. Yoga is a form of physical and mental exercise that involves stretching, balance and meditation. These days I try to do it twice a week, usually in the evening after work, because it helps me relax and sleep better.
>
> I first started yoga about five years ago, when a friend invited me to a class. At the time, I was quite stiff and stressed, and I remember feeling awkward because I could not touch my toes. But after a few weeks, I noticed that I was more flexible and much calmer. I actually did it every day for about two years.
>
> As for why it is important to me, it is not just about fitness. It gives me a quiet part of the day when I can switch off my phone and focus on my breathing. In the future, I would like to do it four or five times a week because I am getting older and I want to stay flexible. Looking back, yoga has probably been the most consistent healthy habit I have ever had.

## If you blank

1. Repeat the question in your own words.
2. Say: *"One example that comes to mind is…"*
3. Start with the **past** memory; it is easier to describe.
4. Add a feeling: *"I remember feeling…"*
5. Close with *"That is why…"*

## Checklist

- [ ] I answered **every sub-question**.
- [ ] I used one idea, not three unrelated ones.
- [ ] I used **present, past and future** tenses.
- [ ] I included a specific memory or example.
- [ ] I used at least two transition phrases.
- [ ] I spoke for **80–90 seconds**.
- [ ] I did not repeat the same sentence in different words.
