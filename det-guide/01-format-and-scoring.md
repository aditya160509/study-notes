# 01 — Format and Scoring

## Key facts

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Length | ~60 minutes |
| Where | At home, on a computer, proctored by webcam + phone |
| Cost | $70 USD per attempt (bundles sometimes cheaper) |
| Results | Usually within 48 hours |
| Validity | 2 years |
| Scale | 10–160 in 5-point steps |
| Retakes | You can buy up to 3 tests in any rolling 30-day period |
| Sharing | Free and unlimited to institutions |

## Structure

| Part | Time | Content | Counts? |
|------|------|---------|---------|
| 1. Setup | ~5 min | ID check, webcam, phone camera via DET mobile app, room scan, ear scan, rules | No |
| 2. Adaptive test | ~45 min | All graded question types, mixed order, difficulty adapts to you | Yes |
| 3. Samples | ~10 min | Writing Sample + Speaking Sample | Graded and sent to universities as raw video/text |

Some prep sites claim the Samples aren't scored. That's wrong: the official Question Guide lists both as scored (Writing and Speaking subscores), and they are also sent to schools. Schools only see the Samples from the test you send them.

## Scoring

The overall score combines all graded questions. You also get **8 subscores**:

### Integrated subscores

| Subscore | Combines | Main question types |
|----------|----------|---------------------|
| Literacy | Reading + Writing | Read and Select, Fill in the Blanks, Read and Complete, Listen and Type, Write About the Photo, Interactive Writing |
| Comprehension | Reading + Listening | Fill in the Blanks, Read and Complete, Interactive Reading, Listen and Type, Interactive Listening |
| Conversation | Listening + Speaking | Interactive Listening, Interactive Speaking, Speak About the Photo |
| Production | Writing + Speaking | Write About the Photo, Interactive Writing, all speaking tasks, Samples |

### Individual subscores (added July 2024)

Reading, Writing, Listening, Speaking.

### How writing and speaking are graded

Official criteria:

- **Writing:** Content, Discourse coherence, Grammar, Lexis (vocabulary)
- **Speaking:** the same four, plus **Fluency** and **Pronunciation**

What that means in practice:
- **Content** — answer the exact prompt; develop it with reasons and examples
- **Discourse coherence** — logical order, linkers, clear structure
- **Grammar** — accuracy and a range of sentence types
- **Lexis** — range and precision of vocabulary
- Duolingo's own tip for speaking tasks is to **use all your time**; more relevant language gives more evidence

Other scoring facts from the official guide:
- Read and Select, Fill in the Blanks, Listen and Respond, most Interactive Reading questions: right/wrong.
- Read and Complete: each word scored separately; spelling must be correct.
- Listen and Type and Listen and Complete: partial credit, the closer the better.
- Highlight the Answer: partial credit for close highlights.

## 01.01 The test minute by minute

The official total is about **60 minutes**, but the clock behaves differently in each phase. Plan for 70 minutes from login to logout.

| Phase | Clock | What you control | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|
| Check-in | ~5 min, not scored | ID, camera, phone pairing, room scan, ear scan | Follow prompts slowly; do not rush the scans |
| Adaptive section | ~45 min, scored | Every answer | Keep moving; there is no going back |
| Writing Sample | 30 s prep + 5 min | The essay | Plan for 30 s, write 140–180 words, proofread 45 s |
| Speaking Sample | 30 s prep + 3 min | The recording | Use 2–2.5 min of structured speech |
| Upload / survey | ~1–2 min | Nothing | Wait for the confirmation screen |

**Do not plan a hard stop at 60 minutes.** A slow room scan or a camera reset can add several minutes; leave buffer before an appointment.

## 01.02 What "adaptive" actually means

The DET is **multi-stage adaptive**, not a simple "one wrong answer drops you" test.

- Questions are selected from a pool based on your running performance.
- A harder question usually means the system is probing your ceiling — it is **not** punishment.
- The section mixes skills, so a weak reading question is followed by writing or speaking, not a run of reading.
- You cannot skip backwards or change an answer.
- Accuracy on medium questions matters more than guessing on very hard ones, but **never leave a blank** — an unanswered item cannot earn credit.

**Practical consequence:** do not try to "game" difficulty. Answer every item at a steady pace. If a question feels impossible, give your best partial answer and move on; the next item may be easier.

## 01.03 Scoring: what is actually measured

### Overall score

- Scale: **10–160**, reported in **5-point increments**.
- The overall score is not an average of the eight subscores; it is derived from performance across all scored tasks.
- Results are usually available **within 48 hours**, sometimes faster.
- Scores are **valid for 2 years** and can be shared free with unlimited institutions.

### The eight subscores

| Integrated subscore | Built from | Tasks that feed it most |
|---|---|---|
| **Literacy** | Reading + Writing | Read and Select, Fill in the Blanks, Read and Complete, Listen and Type, Write About the Photo, Interactive Writing |
| **Comprehension** | Reading + Listening | Fill in the Blanks, Read and Complete, Interactive Reading, Listen and Type, Interactive Listening |
| **Conversation** | Listening + Speaking | Interactive Listening, Interactive Speaking, Speak About the Photo |
| **Production** | Writing + Speaking | Write About the Photo, Interactive Writing, all speaking tasks, Samples |

| Single-skill subscore | Fed by |
|---|---|
| **Reading** | Read and Select, Fill in the Blanks, Read and Complete, Interactive Reading |
| **Writing** | Write About the Photo, Interactive Writing, Interactive Listening summary, Writing Sample |
| **Listening** | Listen and Type, Interactive Listening (including the summary) |
| **Speaking** | Speak About the Photo, Read Then Speak, Interactive Speaking, Speaking Sample |

**Diagnostic use:** if your overall score stalls, compare subscores. Low Literacy with strong Comprehension usually means writing accuracy, not reading speed. Low Production with strong Literacy usually means speaking fluency or essay structure.

### How each task is scored

| Task | Scoring | What that means for you |
|---|---|---|
| Read and Select | Right/wrong per word | Guess rather than time out |
| Fill in the Blanks | Right/wrong; exact spelling | One wrong letter loses the mark |
| Read and Complete | Each word separately; exact spelling | Partial credit is real — attempt every gap |
| Interactive Reading | Right/wrong, except Highlight the Answer (partial) | Highlight only the answering phrase |
| Listen and Type | Partial credit by closeness | Get word order and small words right |
| Listen and Complete | Partial credit by closeness of meaning | Spelling is secondary; idea is primary |
| Listen and Respond | Right/wrong per turn | Tone and relevance decide it |
| Summarize the Conversation | Writing criteria + listening accuracy | Full sentences, accurate content |
| Write About the Photo | Writing criteria | Detail and range, not a list |
| Interactive Writing | Writing criteria, both parts together | Part 2 must add new ideas |
| Speak About the Photo | Speaking criteria | Use the full 90 s |
| Read, Then Speak | Speaking criteria | Cover every bullet |
| Interactive Speaking | Speaking criteria | Direct answer + reason + example |
| Writing Sample | Writing criteria; sent to schools | Treat as a mini application essay |
| Speaking Sample | Speaking criteria; sent to schools | Treat as a recorded interview answer |

### Writing criteria, decoded

| Criterion | What earns credit | What loses credit |
|---|---|---|
| **Content** | Directly answers the exact question; relevant development | Off-topic, memorized, or only partially answers |
| **Discourse coherence** | Clear order, topic sentences, linkers, logical paragraphing | Jumping between ideas, no conclusion, random connectives |
| **Grammar** | Accurate simple sentences **and** some complex structures | Repeated subject–verb, tense, article errors |
| **Lexis** | Precise, varied vocabulary; correct collocations | Basic words repeated, wrong word forms |

### Speaking criteria, decoded

Same four criteria plus:

| Criterion | What earns credit | What loses credit |
|---|---|---|
| **Fluency** | Steady pace, natural pauses, few false starts | Long silences, repeated "um", restarts |
| **Pronunciation** | Clear vowels/consonants, word stress, understandable at first listen | Mumbling, flat intonation, unclear endings |

**Important:** pronunciation is judged on **clarity**, not on sounding American or British. A clear accent is not penalised.

## 01.04 Score interpretation and target setting

| DET | ≈ IELTS | ≈ TOEFL iBT | CEFR | Typical use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 155–160 | 8.0–9.0 | 117–120 | C2 | Top US/UK programs |
| 145–150 | 7.5–8.0 | 109–116 | C1 | Selective universities |
| 135–140 | 7.0–7.5 | 104–108 | C1 | Strong competitive profile |
| 125–130 | 6.5–7.0 | 93–103 | B2–C1 | Common postgraduate minimum |
| 115–120 | 6.0–6.5 | 82–92 | B2 | Many undergraduate pathways |
| 100–110 | 5.5–6.0 | 65–81 | B2 | Foundation / pathway programs |
| 90–95 | 5.0–5.5 | 53–64 | B1 | Pre-sessional English |

**How to set a target:**
1. Find the university's **overall** minimum and any **subscore** minimums.
2. Add **5–10 points** of buffer for a competitive application.
3. Check whether the program accepts the DET at all, and whether it requires a specific subscore (some business or journalism programs do).
4. Do not assume a 130 overall is safe if the school requires 125 in **Production** and you have 120.

## 01.05 Score report, sharing, and retakes

- **Sharing is free and unlimited.** You choose which institutions receive each result.
- Schools see the **Writing Sample and Speaking Sample** from the test you send — not from other attempts.
- You can buy up to **3 tests in a rolling 30-day period**. A "test bundle" lowers the per-test price; the standard single-test price in 2026 is about **$70 USD** (a 2-test bundle is commonly about **$118**).
- A purchased **test credit is valid for 21 days**. Do not buy months before you are ready; you may lose the credit.
- Use **one account only**. Sharing an account is prohibited, and each test-taker needs their own.
- If a result is **uncertified**, you receive a reason and can retake. Common causes are room-scan problems, a phone camera out of position, another person in the room, or background audio.
- **Do not book back-to-back attempts** hoping to superscore. There is no superscoring; each score stands alone. Retake only after fixing the specific weakness.

## 01.07 Frequently asked questions

**Is the DET easier than IELTS or TOEFL?**
It is shorter and tests different task types, not "easier". The reading vocabulary is wide, the speaking is spontaneous, and the writing clock is tight. Many test-takers find it less stressful but harder to game.

**Can I use paper or a pen?**
No. The desk must be clear; notes are not allowed during the test.

**Can I wear headphones?**
No. Headphones, earbuds, and audio devices are prohibited. You must use **speakers** and pass the **ear scan**.

**Do I need a phone?**
Yes. Since late 2025 the **DET mobile app** is required as a **secondary camera**, and the phone performs the room scan and ear scan. It must stay in place for the whole test.

**Can I use Chrome?**
The official setup now specifies the **DET desktop app**. Follow the instructions in your check-in flow; do not assume a browser-only setup.

**Are the Samples really scored?**
Yes. Both the Writing Sample and Speaking Sample are scored and sent to schools. The official Question Guide lists them as scored tasks.

**Can I cancel or reschedule?**
There is no appointment to reschedule — you start when you are ready. Check the current refund policy before purchasing; it can change.

**What if my internet drops?**
Do not close the window or leave the camera. Follow the on-screen recovery instructions. If the test cannot continue, contact support; do not immediately buy another test.
