# 10 — Practice Prompts

Set a timer that matches the real task for every prompt.

## Interactive Writing (5 min + 3 min follow-up)
1. Should university education be free? → *Who should pay for it instead?*
2. Is social media more harmful than helpful for teenagers? → *What rules should platforms follow?*
3. Should students wear school uniforms? → *How do uniforms affect creativity?*
4. Is it better to work in a team or alone? → *Describe how to solve conflict in a team.*
5. Should homework be banned? → *What could replace homework?*
6. Is online learning as effective as classroom learning? → *What makes an online course successful?*
7. Should governments tax sugary drinks? → *What else could reduce obesity?*
8. Are video games good for young people? → *How much gaming is too much?*
9. Should cities ban cars from their centers? → *How would this affect local businesses?*
10. Is it important to learn history? → *Which historical event should every student learn?*
11. Should everyone learn to code? → *How should schools teach it?*
12. Is tourism good for local communities? → *How can tourism be made sustainable?*

## Read, Then Speak / Speaking Sample (up to 90 s / 1–3 min)

2. Describe a place you'd love to visit. Where? Why? What would you do?
3. Talk about a tradition in your country.
4. Describe a time you disagreed with someone. What happened? How was it resolved?
5. Talk about your favorite way to relax.
6. Describe a goal you're working toward.
7. Talk about a teacher who made a difference.
8. Describe a memorable meal.
9. Is it better to save money or spend it on experiences?
10. Describe a piece of technology you couldn't live without.
11. Talk about a time you worked in a group.
12. What would you do with a free year?

## Interactive Speaking sets (35 s each)
**Set A — Studying**
1. Where do you usually study?
2. Why does that place work for you?
3. Do you prefer studying alone or with friends?
4. How do you stay focused?
5. What would you change about how you study?
6. What advice would you give a new student?

**Set B — Technology**
1. How much time do you spend on your phone daily?
2. What do you mostly use it for?
3. Has technology made people more or less social?
4. Should children have smartphones?
5. What technology will be important in 10 years?
6. Would you ever take a week off from technology?

**Set C — Travel**
1. Tell me about a trip you've taken.
2. What did you enjoy most?
3. Do you prefer planned trips or spontaneous ones?
4. How can travel change a person?
5. What are the downsides of tourism?
6. Where would you go next and why?

**Set D — Future**
1. What do you want to study at university?
2. What first got you interested in it?
3. What challenges do you expect?
4. How will you prepare?
5. Where do you see yourself in ten years?
6. What could make you change your plans?

## 10.07 Full mock set B (about 60 minutes)

This set uses the official practice test for reading/listening, then adds your own writing/speaking.

1. Free official practice test (reading/listening sections).
2. One Writing Sample: *Should universities require students to study abroad?*
3. One Speaking Sample: *Describe a time you had to work with someone difficult.*
4. One Interactive Writing: *Should cities ban private cars from the centre?* → follow-up: *How would this affect people with disabilities?*
5. Two photo descriptions (one indoor, one outdoor).
6. One Interactive Listening summary from a 3-minute office-hours clip.

**Review:** compare your writing against the model answers. Count:
- complex sentences per paragraph,
- repeated basic words,
- article/tense errors,
- filler words per minute in speaking.

## 10.08 Self-scoring rubric

### Writing (1–5 each)

| Score | Content | Coherence | Grammar | Lexis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Fully answers; specific development | Clear structure; seamless linkers | Accurate; varied complex sentences | Precise, natural, varied |
| 4 | Answers; some development | Clear; occasional gaps | Few errors; some range | Good range; minor imprecision |
| 3 | Partly answers; general | Understandable; mechanical linkers | Noticeable errors; simple range | Adequate; repetitive |
| 2 | Off-topic or thin | Hard to follow | Frequent errors | Basic; wrong forms |
| 1 | Minimal | No structure | Errors block meaning | Very limited |

### Speaking (1–5 each)

| Score | Content | Coherence | Fluency | Grammar | Lexis | Pronunciation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Fully answers; develops | Clear signposting | Steady; natural pauses | Accurate; varied | Precise | Clear; good stress |
| 4 | Answers; some detail | Mostly clear | Minor hesitation | Few errors | Good range | Mostly clear |
| 3 | Partly answers | Some order | Noticeable pauses | Simple but understandable | Adequate | Understandable with effort |
| 2 | Thin/off-topic | Rambling | Frequent stops | Frequent errors | Limited | Hard to follow |
| 1 | Minimal | None | Long silence | Blocks meaning | Very limited | Unintelligible |

**Target for 130+:** mostly 4s, with at least one 5 in your strongest area.
