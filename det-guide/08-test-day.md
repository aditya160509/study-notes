# 08 — Test Day

## Equipment
- Computer (Windows or macOS) with webcam, microphone and **speakers** — headphones and earbuds are **not allowed**
- Smartphone with the **DET mobile app** (required since Dec 2025) as the second camera, charged
- Passport or government photo ID (check the accepted list for your country)
- Stable internet; laptop plugged in
- The **DET desktop app** (the official setup flow uses the app; do not assume a browser-only setup)
- A stable surface for the phone, positioned to your side with the screen and keyboard in view
- Only **one keyboard, one mouse, and one phone** connected
- Predictive text, proofreading, typing suggestions, and reference tools disabled
- No VPN and no virtual machine

## Room
- Alone, door closed, quiet
- Desk clear: no paper, pens, books, second screen
- Good front-facing light; face and **both ears visible** (no hats, hair over ears, hoods)
- You'll do a **room scan** and **ear scan** with your phone

## Rules that get tests invalidated
- Looking away from the screen repeatedly
- Anyone else entering or speaking in the room
- Wearing headphones/earbuds
- Speaking, reading aloud, or mouthing words when the task does not ask you to speak
- Using other apps, copy-paste, Grammarly, browser extensions
- Leaving the camera frame or leaving your seat
- Touching or moving the phone once it is set up as the secondary camera
- Phone camera not showing the screen/keyboard properly
- Typing or speaking when you are not instructed to
- Taking notes or recording questions
- Using a VPN, virtual machine, or shared account
- Using software with predictive text, proofreading, or typing suggestions
- Memorized/off-topic answers can be flagged

## Checklist
- [ ] ID ready
- [ ] Phone charged, DET app installed and logged in
- [ ] Phone stand or stack to position the second camera
- [ ] Computer charged and plugged in; updates done
- [ ] All other apps and notifications closed
- [ ] Room quiet, desk clear, door shut
- [ ] Light in front of you; ears uncovered
- [ ] Drink water before starting; keep the desk clear
- [ ] Bathroom before starting (no breaks)
- [ ] All study notes and files closed and out of sight

## During the test
- Hard questions = good sign. Keep going.
- Use the full time on writing and speaking.
- Proofread written answers.
- In Interactive Speaking, listen fully before recording.
- If tech fails, don't leave the screen; follow the on-screen instructions.

## After the test
- Results usually in ≤48 hours (check email).
- If a test is not certified, you'll get a reason; you can retake (within the 3-per-30-days limit).
- Share scores free from your dashboard to each university.

## 08.01 The official setup, step by step

The DET now uses a **computer + smartphone** setup. The phone is not optional.

### Step 1 — Prepare the computer

- Install the **DET desktop app** before test day.
- Run a practice test or the equipment check to confirm camera, microphone, and speakers.
- Close every other application: messaging, email, Grammarly, screen recorders, cloud sync clients.
- Disable notifications and automatic updates.
- Plug in the charger.
- Use a wired or stable Wi-Fi connection. Have a phone hotspot as backup, but do not switch mid-test unless instructed.

### Step 2 — Prepare the phone

- Install the **DET mobile app**.
- Charge it and keep it plugged in if possible.
- Connect it to the same Wi-Fi network.
- Put it on a stable stand or a stack of books — **not in your hand**.
- Position it to your **side**, with your computer screen, keyboard, and hands visible.
- Do not use third-party camera apps; the DET app must control the camera.

### Step 3 — Prepare the room

- Alone for the entire test, door closed.
- Desk clear: computer, phone, ID only.
- No paper, pens, books, notes, headphones, smartwatch, or second screen.
- Plain background; front-facing light; no strong backlight.
- Face and **both ears** visible: tie back long hair, remove hats and hoods.
- Remove **glasses** and **clip-on or magnetic earrings** before the ear scan, as required by the official rules.
- Silence other devices and ask household members not to enter.

### Step 4 — Check-in

1. Launch the DET desktop app and log in.
2. Scan the QR code with your phone to connect the secondary camera.
3. Complete the **room scan**: slowly show the desk, walls, floor, and surrounding area.
4. Complete the **ear scan**: briefly touch the phone to each ear to prove no earbuds are worn.
5. Show your **ID** clearly.
6. Read and accept the rules.
7. Keep the phone in its position for the entire test.

### Step 5 — During the test

- Keep your face, hands, and keyboard in view of both cameras.
- Look at the screen, not around the room.
- Do not talk to yourself or read questions aloud.
- Do not touch the phone.
- If a technical warning appears, follow the on-screen instructions and stay in frame.

### Step 6 — After the test

- Wait for the upload confirmation before closing the app.
- Check your email and dashboard for the result (usually within 48 hours).
- Share the score with institutions from the dashboard; sharing is free.

## 08.02 Secondary camera positioning

The most common setup failure is a phone that does not show enough of the workspace.

**Correct position:**

- Phone to the **side** of the computer, slightly behind your shoulder.
- Lens at roughly **desk height or slightly above**.
- Screen, keyboard, hands, and face all visible.
- Stable surface; cable secured so it cannot pull the phone.
- No bright window directly behind the phone.

**Incorrect position:**

- Phone in front of you, blocking the screen.
- Phone behind you, showing only your back.
- Phone too low, showing only the ceiling.
- Phone held in your hand.
- Phone showing the screen but not your hands.

**Test it before check-in:** open the DET app's preview and ask: can I see the whole keyboard and both hands? If not, move the phone.

## 08.03 Room scan and ear scan

### Room scan

- Move slowly. Fast panning creates blur and may fail review.
- Show the desk surface, under the desk, the walls, and the door.
- Open the door if instructed; show the hallway is empty.
- Show any reflective surfaces that could hide a screen.
- Do not skip corners to save time; an incomplete scan is a common cause of uncertified results.

### Ear scan

- Tie back hair before starting.
- Hold the phone close to one ear, then the other, as prompted.
- Remove earrings or large accessories that could be mistaken for earbuds.
- Do not wear a smartwatch, earbuds, or a headset at any point.

## 08.04 Allowed vs prohibited

| Allowed | Prohibited |
|---|---|
| Computer with webcam, mic, speakers | Headphones, earbuds, headsets |
| Smartphone running the DET app | Second monitor or tablet |
| One keyboard and one mouse | Paper, pens, notes, books |
| ID document | Smartwatch, smart glasses, fitness band |
| Plain desk and chair | VPN or virtual machine |
| Water in a clear, label-free container (check current rules) | Food, gum, tobacco |
| Tissues | Other people or pets in the room |
| Closed door | Leaving the room mid-test |

**If in doubt, remove it.** A cleared desk is faster to scan and easier to certify.

## 08.05 Minute-by-minute test day

| Time | Action |
|---|---|
| T–60 min | Eat, hydrate, bathroom; no heavy caffeine |
| T–45 min | Set up computer and phone; close apps; silence notifications |
| T–30 min | Position phone; test camera preview; clear desk |
| T–20 min | Check ID, lighting, ears visible; final room check |
| T–10 min | Sit down; slow breathing; review your one-page strategy |
| T–0 | Start check-in; follow scans slowly |
| During | Steady pace; never leave blanks; speak clearly |
| End | Wait for upload confirmation |
| +48 h | Check results; share with schools |

**Do not** study new material in the last hour. It increases anxiety and rarely improves performance.

## 08.06 If something goes wrong

### Internet drops

- Do not close the app or leave the camera.
- Follow the on-screen reconnection instructions.
- If the test cannot resume, note the time and contact support.
- Do not immediately purchase another test; support may restore or advise you.

### Camera or microphone fails

- Stay seated and in frame.
- Use the app's device-check flow if prompted.
- If the failure is unrecoverable, contact support with your test ID.
- A test cancelled for lost camera access may be eligible for a replacement; ask before repurchasing.

### Phone disconnects

- Do not pick it up and walk around.
- Follow the prompt to reconnect; keep the phone in roughly the same position.
- If it cannot reconnect, the test may be uncertified. Contact support.

### Another person enters

- Do not speak to them.
- The test may be flagged. You will be asked to retake; do not argue with the proctor.

### You need to cough or sneeze

- Turn away briefly, then return to frame. Do not leave the seat.
- Repeated interruptions may be flagged; mention it to support if the result is delayed.

## 08.07 Why results get uncertified

| Reason | Prevention |
|---|---|
| Another person visible or audible | Test alone; warn household members |
| Phone camera out of position | Stable stand; preview before starting |
| Incomplete room scan | Slow, full 360° scan |
| Ear scan failed | Tie back hair; remove earbuds/earrings |
| Looking away repeatedly | Keep eyes on screen |
| Reading aloud or mouthing | Read silently |
| Using a second device | Remove all extra devices |
| Copy-paste or external help | Disable clipboard tools; work independently |
| Leaving fullscreen or clicking other windows | Close all apps before starting |
| Memorized/off-topic answers | Use structures, not scripts |

**If uncertified:** read the reason carefully. Fix the specific cause before retaking. A retake without fixing the cause usually produces the same outcome.

## 08.08 After the result

- **Certified:** share it. Check whether each school needs the score sent directly from the dashboard.
- **Lower than target:** compare subscores, not just the overall. Decide whether a retake is worth it given the 3-per-30-days limit.
- **Much lower than practice:** suspect a setup or timing problem. Review your mock conditions and fix them before retaking.
- **Writing/Speaking Sample concerns:** remember schools see the raw sample. If it does not represent you, a retake may be worthwhile even if the overall score is acceptable.

**Retake rule:** retake only when you have a **specific fix**, not simply because you hope for a better day.

**Credit rule:** a test credit is valid for **21 days** from purchase. Do not buy a bundle far in advance unless you are certain you will use it within that window.
