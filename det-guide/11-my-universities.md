## India campuses: optional or required?

English proof is **required** at all four, but it doesn't have to be a test. Your school results cover York, Aberdeen and Illinois Tech. Bristol Mumbai is the only one to ask about.

## What you actually need

- **No test needed:** 14 (MIT, Stanford, Georgia Tech, UW, Virginia Tech, UT Dallas, ASU, UCD, Edinburgh, Glasgow, York Mumbai, Aberdeen Mumbai, Illinois Tech Mumbai, plus UIUC if you send SAT).
- **Confirm by email first:** UIUC, Purdue, UMass, Trinity, St Andrews, Strathclyde, Heriot-Watt, Bristol Mumbai.
- **Test definitely needed:** UMD, and all 3 Australian universities.

**Recommendation:** UMD alone can be covered by the **DET (120+)**. Australia needs **IELTS Academic** (the DET won't work for the visa). If you're keeping the Australian schools, take IELTS Academic once (aim 7.0, nothing below 6.5); it also covers UMD and every "confirm" school. If you drop Australia, the DET is enough.

Requirements change; confirm on each official page before paying for a test.
