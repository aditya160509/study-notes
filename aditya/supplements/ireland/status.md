# Ireland: Application Writing Status

## Trinity College Dublin

No universal undergraduate supplement is currently confirmed. The application route and statement requirement depend on fee status, education system, and exact course. Once Computer Science and the direct/CAO route are confirmed, copy any portal prompt here verbatim.

## University College Dublin

Non-EU applicants generally apply directly, and document requirements vary by program. No universal undergraduate personal essay is currently confirmed. Do not send a Common App-style essay unless the portal asks for one.

## Material to use if requested

Use the academic direction from `../uk/ucas.md`: computational systems, testable mechanisms, Further Mathematics, IPQ, independent building, and professional exposure. Avoid the Common App’s personal narrative unless the prompt explicitly asks for personal growth.
