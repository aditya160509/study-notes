# MIT Research Notes

## Exact academic matches

The Laboratory for Financial Engineering is the closest institutional match for GASI, NEXUS, Future Lab, and the investor-attention paper. MIT describes its work as the quantitative analysis of financial markets through mathematical, statistical, and computational methods. Its current areas include asset-market dynamics, investor behavior, systemic risk, and financial applications of machine learning.

Andrew Lo is the clearest faculty connection. His work on investor behavior and adaptive markets overlaps with Aditya's questions about attention, volatility, imitation, and simulated traders.

The Center for Deployable Machine Learning is the strongest match for Glassbox. Its stated goal is machine-learning systems that remain robust, reliable, safe, and understandable when deployed.
