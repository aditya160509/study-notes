## Exact academic matches

The Stanford Center for Computational Market Design is the cleanest match for Future Lab, GASI, and NEXUS. It combines algorithm design, economics, machine learning, and operations research to study modern market platforms.

Susan Athey's research connects machine learning, econometrics, marketplace design, platform markets, and technology for social impact. This is a genuine bridge between Aditya's financial research and his interest in systems that influence human choices.

Stanford HAI is relevant to Glassbox and PhenoSync because it studies reliable systems designed around human use and social consequences.
