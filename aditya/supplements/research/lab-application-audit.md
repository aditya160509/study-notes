## Rejected

### Adding an MIT professor or laboratory to the field response

The live MIT wording asks why the field appeals to Aditya. The draft already answers that within 145 words. A faculty name would remove space from his market question and make the response sound like a compressed Why MIT essay.

### Adding faculty names to Stanford

Stanford's present prompts ask about learning, contribution, and personal life. The FPL essay demonstrates alignment with computational market design without borrowing a center's description.

### Turning every short answer into intellectual evidence

Football, pickleball, Dakshinayan, sleep, lost keys, and family calls stay ordinary. The application already contains enough research.

### Using a supposedly impressive historical event

India's 1991 reforms were rejected because Aditya has no personal interest or family connection to the event. Michael Jackson's 1996 Mumbai concert is now the lead answer because it joins a real musical preference with his city.

### Treating third-party examples as formulas

The lab's sample essays are useful for diagnosing specificity, movement, and reflection. Their sentence shapes and dramatic devices are not templates to copy. Official prompts, factual accuracy, and Aditya's speaking voice take priority.

## Current ceiling judgment

The academic essays already meet the lab's strongest tests. Further improvement now depends mainly on verified human material, not additional research terminology or university names.
