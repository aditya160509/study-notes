# Honors (Common App)

Common App allows 5 honors, each up to 100 characters, with a grade level (9–12 only) and a recognition level (school, state/regional, national or international).

| # | Honor (max 100 characters) | Grade | Level |

| 1 | IAYP Silver Award (International Award for Young People): 26-week quant research skills project | 11 | International |

## Checked and left out

- **7th Youth Football Championship, Goa 2025:** no position is ticked on the certificate, so it's participation only.
- **Futurum 2nd place (JBCN school fest, Grade 9, 2023–24):** a school class activity; too weak for Honors.
- **Intramural handball/volleyball 2nd place, Innovators' Convention certificate:** these are participation or intramural results; leave them out.
- **Silverzone Science Olympiad (Class 2, 2016):** too early to count.
- **Cambridge IPQ 9980:** this is a graded qualification, not an honor, so it goes with your courses.
