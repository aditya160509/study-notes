## Additional Information: Challenges (250 words max)

My grandfather was seriously ill during my February/March 2026 exams, and he died shortly before my May/June exams. We were very close, and it was the first time I had lost someone in my family. I sat the February/March papers knowing his condition was getting worse, and went into May/June still grieving. I could not concentrate the way I normally do.

At the same time I was carrying too much. I was taking six subjects, including Further Mathematics, Physics and Economics, while also running two research papers through journal submission and building several software projects. That load was already too much, and with my grandfather's illness and death on top of it, my AS results came in lower than the marks I had kept steady all year.

The grades hit my confidence harder than I expected, and it took me some time to recover. Looking back, I had said yes to too many big things at once. Since then I have dropped Economics so I can give my remaining subjects and my research the attention they need.

I am sharing this so my AS results can be read with that context.
