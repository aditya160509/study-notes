## Against each essay

**Hsiao (9.6).** Her "In our house" refrain appears at the start, the middle and the end, and the snake/snack image returns in the last lines. Your mūḍhamate does this at the start and the end, but it disappears for the three research paragraphs. Her emotional peak is a scene: her mother crying, and "We were both crying now." Yours is a summary ("For days I looked for reasons…"). You beat her on intellectual depth. Her essay shows almost no thinking about ideas, and yours shows a mind correcting itself.

**Altenburg (8.9).** His strength is the feeling of being inside his head. He lets odd thoughts in, like Stravinsky or the Sunset Lanes "Las Vegas", without explaining them. Your voice is controlled rather than spontaneous, and that's the right call for a story built on cause and effect. He loses points for a wandering middle and a stated ending. Your ending ("I let him finish") is clearly stronger.

**Andrian (8.6).** Her first 200 words are the best scene in any of the four essays: the garbage pile, "Bota, bota, matava," and a shout she regrets instantly. She admits something genuinely ugly, the way you admit "I heard 'use it.'" But she then states the lesson ("Education is an opportunity, not a burden") and closes with "my actions at Harvard." You never state a moral, and that's why you finish ahead of her.

## What separates 9.1 from 9.6

1. **One scene shown in real time.** The revision moment needs a real detail: the section's name, what you did before deleting it, and the page count before and after. (Q1 in `questions-for-aditya.md`.) That one change is worth about +0.3.
2. **The hymn in the middle.** One small appearance during the research, for example "I had the hymn on while I cut," but only if it's true. About +0.1–0.2.
3. **Facts verified.** These include the exact reviewer quotes, the notification wording, whether you really "never stopped to ask," and "tipping point" versus "threshold." This adds nothing to the score, but any error costs credibility.

Do **not** add the Hindi line, the 1.39 figure, more testing detail or a stated lesson. Each one would pull the essay toward Andrian's second half.

# Full question list for the Common App essay

The examples are **made up** and only show the level of detail needed. Answer only with what is true, and write "don't remember" when you can't.

**Opening (Andrian)**
- **Q1.** On the stairs, what did a friend actually say when you opened it? Did anyone read it aloud?
  > *Example:* Rohan read "We regret to inform you" out loud before I could stop him.
- **Q2.** What did you do in the first 10 seconds after reading "Rejected"? Lock the phone, laugh, walk off?
  > *Example:* I locked the phone, said "it's fine," and walked to the next class without saying anything else.

**Scene at the peak (Hsiao/Andrian)**
- **Q3.** The cut: the time, the place, the section's name, how big it was, and what you did right before deleting it. Did you copy it somewhere, or scroll through it one last time?
  > *Example:* 11 pm, my desk. "Robustness by region": 4 tables, 6 pages. I saved it as "maybe later.docx," then deleted it from the paper.
- **Q4.** What was the paper's length before and after (pages or words)?
  > *Example:* 34 pages to 23.

**Vulnerability (Andrian/Hsiao)**
- **Q5.** When your brother saw you use AI without understanding it, did he ever say anything? What exactly?
  > *Example:* He saw my notes and said "you can't explain one line of this."
- **Q6.** When the first journal desk-rejected you, did you tell your teacher? What did he say or do?
  > *Example:* I didn't tell him for a week. When I did, he just asked "Did you read their scope?"

**Thread (Hsiao)**
- **Q7.** During the research or the revision, was the hymn, your father or the word mūḍhamate ever actually there? Was it playing while you worked, did your father say something, or did you think of the word?
  > *Example:* While I was cutting, Appa called from the hall, "Still working?", and the hymn was playing in the car downstairs.

**Voice (Altenburg)**
- **Q8.** That night, or during the revision, did you have one strange or funny thought you still remember?
  > *Example:* I wondered whether the reviewer had read my introduction twice or given up halfway.

**Facts (needed before submitting)**
- **Q9.** Copy the exact notification subject and the three reviewer quotes from the decision email. Also: before the first submission, had you looked at the journal's requirements at all? Answer yes, no, or "skimmed."
- **Q10.** Ending echo: after resubmitting, where do you check the portal (phone, stairs, bed)? And is there a real recent car ride where your father explained mūḍhamate?
  > *Example:* I check it on my phone between classes, usually on the same stairs.
- **Q11.** The paper's threshold: was there one level for all 21 markets, or did it differ by market? This decides between "tipping point" and "threshold."

The supplement questions are unchanged (Q4–Q16 in `supplements/questions-for-aditya.md`).
