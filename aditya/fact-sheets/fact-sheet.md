## Teacher-facing snapshot

Aditya is a curious, unusually self-directed student who learns by building. He is drawn to questions where mathematics, computer science, economics, finance, and human behaviour overlap. Rather than stopping at a classroom exercise, he tends to turn an idea into a working system, test it against real or simulated data, look for weaknesses, and revise it until he can defend the reasoning himself.

His work is distinctive because it combines abstraction with execution. He has written independent research papers, built quantitative-finance and engineering platforms, run simulations, designed educational and sports communities, taught younger students, and worked in a real stock-market research environment. He is not interested in technology as a badge; he wants to understand why a system works and whether it survives contact with evidence.

The most important growth story is intellectual ownership. Early in his use of AI tools, he sometimes optimized for producing a finished answer before fully understanding it. A research-paper failure forced him to confront that gap. Since then, he has made a personal rule: tools may help him draft, search, or suggest, but they may not make the final intellectual decision. He must be able to reconstruct and defend the work without assistance. That experience made him more rigorous, more honest about uncertainty, and more willing to persist through null results and rejection.

Aditya is a mathematically ambitious builder-researcher who turns curiosity about markets, AI, climate, engineering, and sport into working systems—and who has grown from seeking fast answers to insisting on understanding every answer he stands behind.

## What he has created

### Research and mathematical work

- **GASI / Global Attention Saturation Index:** A research project measuring investor-attention thresholds across 22 equity markets. Aditya used an instrumental-variable strategy, threshold estimation, cross-sectional corrections, and falsification tests; the paper documents a recurring “Silence Signature” in high-attention regimes. The paper passed desk review at *Borsa Istanbul Review*, a Q1 journal, and completed a peer-review round.
- **Behavioral Ownership Gap:** A paper treating Fantasy Premier League participation as a behavioural market-microstructure problem rather than only a game. It models the gap between optimal and actual player ownership using a large panel of gameweeks and an out-of-sample validation design. The paper is under review at the *Journal of Sports Economics*.
- **NEXUS Exchange:** An agent-based market simulator with nine trader archetypes and 10,200 Monte Carlo simulations. It independently recovered the same structural threshold as GASI and validated nine canonical market “stylized facts,” including fat tails and volatility clustering.
- **Neural-network exposition:** A 12–15-page mathematical paper deriving the Neural Tangent Kernel from first principles using matrices, eigenvalues, diagonalisation, Taylor series, and first-order differential equations accessible from Further Mathematics.
- **PhenoSync:** An open-data climate and phenology pipeline combining GBIF, NASA MODIS, NOAA, and FAOSTAT data. It investigates shifts in biological timing and translates potential mismatch into crop exposure and economic value-at-risk.
- **Cambridge IPQ 9980:** An independent 4,982-word research qualification investigating whether high-frequency trading destabilises markets during periods of stress.

### Software and systems

- **ATLAS / QUANT360:** A full-stack quantitative research platform built from scratch. It includes modules for factors, volatility, regimes, machine learning, causal inference, screening, PCA, and portfolio construction, and is used by a CS graduate for real analysis.
- **Daedalus:** A full-stack engineering optimisation platform built for his STEM Racing team. It supports dimensional validation, provenance tracking, Monte Carlo sensitivity and robustness analysis, Pareto optimisation, scenario comparison, equation traceability, and 3D visualisation. The team uses it for actual design trade-offs.
- **Zeus:** An agentic goal-decomposition pipeline with memory decay, directed-acyclic-graph execution visualisation, browser automation, and a verified end-to-end task loop. The design reduced token use by approximately 35%.
- **Glassbox SRE:** An autonomous incident-response system that connects production alerts to commit analysis, runbook retrieval, Prometheus-based impact measurement, evidence-cited incident briefs, and postmortems. Aditya built an evaluation harness in which the full reasoning system substantially outperformed a deterministic baseline.
- **Future Lab:** A simulated stock market built for the Quant Finance Club. It contains roughly 150 companies across 15 industries, fundamentals-based intrinsic valuation, realistic price drivers, mean reversion, industry-specific scoring, and the ability to branch the market timeline to explore alternate futures.

## Character and working style

- **Deeply self-directed:** He frequently begins with a question no class has assigned and teaches himself the missing material.
- **Persistent:** He has continued research after null results, journal rejection, debugging, and long stretches without visible progress.
- **Intellectually honest:** His essay describes a painful early lesson about submitting work he could not defend. Since then, he has made defensibility and understanding non-negotiable.
- **Systems-minded:** He naturally sees connections between components and builds pipelines, platforms, and repeatable processes rather than isolated demonstrations.
- **Evidence-oriented:** He prefers a claim that survives testing to a clever-sounding idea that has not been checked.
- **Quietly demanding of himself:** He may appear calm or reserved, but he sets a high internal standard and often keeps working after the externally visible requirement has been met.
- **Collaborative through usefulness:** He tends to lead by making a group more capable—by building infrastructure, maintaining consistency, explaining an idea, or doing the unglamorous work that keeps a project moving.
- **Still developing balance:** His ambition can lead him to take on too much and to move faster than his understanding. He is learning to pace himself, communicate earlier, and distinguish productive intensity from unnecessary perfectionism.
