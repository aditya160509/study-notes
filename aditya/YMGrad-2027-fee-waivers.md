# YMGrad 2027 application fee waivers

Source: [Universities Without Application Fee in 2027](https://ymgrad.com/article/universities-waiving-application-fee-2027), updated October 8, 2026; reviewed October 31, 2026. This is a summary of the entire article, not independent verification of the offers. It primarily covers **graduate, master's and MBA applications**, not first-year undergraduate applications. Confirm the program, intake, international eligibility, code and deadline with the university before using any offer.

## All codes printed in the article

| University and program | Code | Article's instructions and limitations |
|---|---|---|
| NYU Tandon School of Engineering, graduate applications | **TANDONBKLYN** | Enter in Supplemental Questions before submission. The article describes this as a graduate waiver; it does not establish eligibility for undergraduate applicants. |
| Culinary Institute of America | **APPLY26** | Enter in the application's fee-waiver section before submitting. The article does not specify the program or confirm Fall 2027 validity; check directly with admissions. |

The article also mentions individual codes provided by Pittsburgh's admissions office and through Ohio State's waiver request process, but does **not** print a reusable code for either.

## Complete university-by-university list

The following descriptions are what YMGrad reports; details and availability can change. Numbering follows the article, which lists Carnegie Mellon twice.

1. **NYU Tandon, graduate engineering:** Use `TANDONBKLYN` in Supplemental Questions before submitting.
2. **University of Pittsburgh, Katz Graduate School of Business:** Eligible approved graduate applicants receive a code from admissions. Enter the individually provided code in the graduate business application; an admissions event may provide more information. The article gives no request form or public code.
3. **Cornell Johnson, MBA:** Eligible applicants or virtual Ask Admissions attendees may receive a waiver. Have an application in progress, register using the same email as the application, and attend the complete virtual session while signed in. The article says attendance is recorded and the waiver generally appears within seven business days; wait for it before submitting. [MBA application FAQ](https://business.cornell.edu/admissions/graduate/mba/application-guide/mba-application-requirements-faqs/).
4. **Dartmouth Thayer, graduate engineering:** Review program-specific waiver eligibility and consider attending an engineering webinar. The article does not promise a waiver or provide a code or form.
5. **Vanderbilt Owen, MBA:** The article says eligible virtual-session participants can receive a waiver. Register for an MBA admissions session, attend in full, and follow the instructions provided.
6. **UVA Darden, full-time MBA:** The article reports a $250 application fee, reduced by $100 for eligible webinars or selected partner events and by $150 for qualifying in-person events. It lists full waivers for some categories, including eligible reapplicants, veterans, first-generation students and certain alumni; the reduction applies only to the applicable cycle. [Darden admissions FAQ](https://www.darden.virginia.edu/mba/admissions/faq).
7. **University of Maryland Graduate School:** Begin the application, complete the [Graduate Application Fee Waiver Request Form](https://gradschool.umd.edu/feewaiverinformation) before the program deadline, and await a decision before submitting. The article says waivers are limited and individually considered, with an otherwise $90 fee.
8. **University of Rochester Simon, MBA:** Attend a live Simon webinar or complete the school's Claim Your Free Waiver form. Follow the admissions instructions. [Simon fee-waiver information](https://simon.rochester.edu/programs/full-time-mba/admissions/admissions-faqs/fee-waiver).
9. **Northeastern, specified MS and graduate certificate programs:** The article claims an automatic waiver for new applicants for Fall 2026 and Spring 2027, without a separate request. Those are the terms stated; the article does not establish Fall 2027 coverage. Check the program. [Graduate admissions](https://camd.northeastern.edu/graduate-admissions/).
10. **University of Minnesota Carlson, MBA:** Contact admissions, schedule an appointment, or attend an MBA event to ask about eligibility. The article does not confirm an automatic waiver.
11. **Indiana University Kelley, Full-Time +Flex MBA:** The article says Fall 2027 Phase 1 has no initial application fee and eligible virtual roundtable attendees can receive Phase 2 waivers. Register and follow admissions instructions.
12. **Baylor Hankamer, MBA:** The article reports fees waived for MBA programs without a code or request. Confirm current availability directly.
13. **SMU Cox, MBA/business programs:** The article reports no application fee, and therefore no code or waiver request. [Admissions requirements](https://www.smu.edu/cox/business-degrees/mba-programs/two-year-mba/admissions/requirements).
14. **Culinary Institute of America:** The article lists `APPLY26` to enter in the fee-waiver section. Ask admissions whether it applies to the intended program and intake.
15. **Carnegie Mellon College of Engineering, graduate:** Individual departments review waivers. Check the intended department's rules, deadlines and required documents. The article names biomedical, chemical, civil and environmental, electrical and computer, mechanical, materials science, engineering and public policy, information networking, and integrated innovation programs; it does not provide department-specific form links.
16. **Brown School of Engineering, graduate:** The article says to request a waiver in the online application's Financial Information section before submission. No separate code or form is specified.
17. **Rice MEML:** The article says attendees of a Master of Engineering Management and Leadership information session are eligible. Check upcoming dates and register through [Rice information sessions](https://epmp.rice.edu/info-sessions#mem).
18. **Harvard Business School, MBA:** Need-based waiver request after starting an application; the article says submit the form at least two weeks before the deadline and allow 7–10 business days for processing. [HBS need-based waiver](https://www.hbs.edu/mba/admissions/application-process/need-based-application-fee-waiver).
19. **Ohio State Fisher, full-time MBA:** The article says eligible applicants can request a promo code using the school's fee-waiver request form and enter it before submission. [Link provided in YMGrad article](https://info.fisher.osu.edu/graduate/smb-a-overview-typ) — verify that this page provides the current form.
20. **Carnegie Mellon College of Engineering, graduate:** A second entry repeats that individual departments handle waiver requests; check the relevant department before submitting.

## Forms, documents and steps mentioned

- **Maryland:** Graduate Application Fee Waiver Request Form, linked above. Complete before the program deadline and wait for a decision.
- **Rochester Simon:** Claim Your Free Waiver form, available through the linked fee-waiver page, or an eligible webinar.
- **Harvard Business School:** Need-based application fee-waiver form, linked from its waiver information page.
- **Ohio State Fisher:** Fee-waiver request form for a code; verify the linked destination and current form.
- **Brown:** Financial Information section within the graduate application.
- **Other supporting evidence:** The article says financial-need waivers can require documentation and that Carnegie Mellon department requirements vary, but it does not enumerate specific documents. Do not assume a particular document is sufficient.

## General guidance in the article

Waivers may be based on financial need, event attendance, academic merit, qualifying programs or school-specific criteria. Some must be approved or entered before submission, and refund availability varies. International eligibility and deadlines differ by program. The FAQ separately mentions a Rice doctoral waiver period and a Stanford graduate request lead time but provides neither a specific code nor full terms for those examples.
