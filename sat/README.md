# SAT Resources

Official College Board resources are organized by purpose:

- `official-practice-tests/` — SAT Practice Tests 4–11, scoring guides, answer explanations, sample questions, and test specifications.
- `study-guides/` — student guide, practice quick-start guide, test-day registration guide, parent guide, practice poster, and practice advice.
- `educator-resources/` — classroom practice guides, teacher implementation guide, educator skill-building guide, specifications overview, and the official practice toolkit.
- `test-day/` — School Day student guide, test-date materials, international dates flyer, and test-day reminder poster.
- `question-bank-and-cheatsheets/` — College Board Student/Educator Question Bank guides and Khan Academy's SAT Math reference-sheet guide.
- `skill-guides/` — College Board content domains and Khan Academy's official Digital SAT preparation overview.
- `additional-practice/` — additional SAT Practice Tests 1–3 and answer explanations.

Cheat sheets in `question-bank-and-cheatsheets/` include math formulas, Reading and Writing rules, and a practical study workflow.

The Student Question Bank and adaptive Bluebook practice tests are official but require College Board/My Practice or the Bluebook app rather than public file downloads.

Useful live resources:

- Student Question Bank: https://satsuitequestionbank.collegeboard.org/digital/search
- Educator Question Bank: https://satsuiteeducatorquestionbank.collegeboard.org/
- Official Digital SAT Prep on Khan Academy: https://www.khanacademy.org/digital-sat
